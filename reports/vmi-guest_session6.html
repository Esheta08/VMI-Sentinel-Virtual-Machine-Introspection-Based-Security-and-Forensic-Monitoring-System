Method and limitations

  • Process events are derived from CR3 (address-space switch) interception through KVMI/LibVMI; no software runs inside the guest.
  • Under heavy load some exec() calls are recorded as PROCESS_CREATED, so exec counts are under-reported; program names in the evidence are still correct.

  • Parent process IDs are not collected yet, so process trees cannot be reconstructed.
  • Detection uses process counts per 30 s window: low-rate activity at a normal process rate is not detected, and benign bursts (e.g. starting scripts) can raise alerts.
  • Alerts mark windows that differ from the training baseline; they are leads for an analyst, not proof of compromise.
